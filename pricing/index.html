Pricing questions

Straight answers.

How much does Putnam Lead Group cost?

Two simple prices. The one-time cleanup is $299. The optional monthly plan is $175 a month. You’ll know the price before any work starts.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The monthly plan is month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

What’s included in the $299 cleanup?

We set up or clean up your Google Business Profile, create or fix your Apple Maps and Bing listings, make your name, phone and website match across the main directories, and tidy your website wording if you have one. You get a plain-English list of what we fixed.

What does the $175 a month plan do?

It keeps everything fresh after the cleanup: review replies, new Google posts from your job photos, one new page or post a month for a service or town you want more of, accurate listings, and a short monthly report.

What’s not included?

Building a brand-new website, paid ads, paid tools or directory upgrades, and any promise of rankings or a number of leads. If you ever need one of those, we’ll quote it first.

Do I keep my accounts?

Yes. Your Google profile, listings, website and social accounts stay in your name. We work as a manager you can remove at any time.
