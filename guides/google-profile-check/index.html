Free guide

The 60-second Google profile check.

See your business the way a new customer does. Grab your phone and answer these ten questions. Every “no” is a spot where a call might go to someone else.

The 10 questions

  1. Search your business name and town. On your phone, search your business name plus your town, like you were a new customer. Does your profile come up, with your correct name?
  2. Check your hours. Are your regular and holiday hours right? Wrong hours are the fastest way to lose a call.
  3. Tap your phone number. Does it call you? Does the website link open the right page?
  4. Look at your services and service area. Does it list the work you actually want, and the towns you actually drive to?
  5. Scroll your photos. Are there real photos of your work from the last few months, or just a logo?
  6. Read your newest reviews. Is the newest one recent? Did every review get a reply?
  7. Search your trade and town. Search something like “roofer Palatka” or “lawn care near me.” Do you show up in the map results?
  8. Ask an AI tool. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity “who’s a good [your trade] in [your town]?” Are you mentioned? Is what it says about you right?
  9. Check Apple Maps. On an iPhone, search your business in Apple Maps. Is it there, and is it right?
  10. Count your “no” answers. Every “no” is a spot where a customer might call someone else instead.
