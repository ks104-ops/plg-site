About

Veteran-owned. Local to Putnam County.

I’m Kyle Simmons, a Marine Corps veteran who lives in the Palatka area. I started Putnam Lead Group to help the local businesses I call on myself get found, get trusted and get the job.

Why I do this

The best tradespeople in our county are usually too busy doing the work to keep up with Google, reviews and messages. Customers can’t hire someone they can’t find, and they won’t wait long for a reply. I fix that part, so you can keep doing the part you’re great at.

How I work

  • You deal with me directly. No call centers and no hand-offs.
  • Plain English. You’ll always know what I did and why.
  • Real reviews only. Never fake, bought or traded for a discount.
  • No guarantees I can’t keep. Nobody can promise #1 on Google, so I won’t.
  • Your accounts stay yours. I work as a manager you can remove at any time.
  • No long contracts. The monthly plan is month to month.
