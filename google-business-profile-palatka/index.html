Check your own profile in 5 minutes

Search your business name on your phone, open your listing, and go down this list. Every “no” is a reason a customer might pass you by.

  1. Hours: are they right today, and did you add holiday hours?
  2. Phone: does tapping Call ring a phone someone answers?
  3. Category: is your main category the job people hire you for, like “Lawn care service” or “HVAC contractor”?
  4. Services: are your actual services listed, with plain names?
  5. Service area: are the towns you work in listed, like Palatka, East Palatka, Interlachen or Crescent City?
  6. Description: does it say what you do, where, and why to call you, in plain words?
  7. Photos: is there a photo from this year? Real job photos beat logos.
  8. Reviews: has every review, good or bad, gotten a reply?
  9. Website link: does it work, and does the site show the same name, phone and towns?
  10. Other listings: do Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and Yelp show the same name, phone and hours?
