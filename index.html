FAQ

Good questions.

How much does Putnam Lead Group cost?

Two simple prices. The one-time cleanup is $299. The optional monthly plan is $175 a month. You’ll know the price before any work starts.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The monthly plan is month to month. To stop, give us 30 days’ written notice, by email or text.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. You keep using your phone the way you do now. We handle the setup and the upkeep.

Do I need a website?

Not to start. Most of what we do runs off your Google Business Profile and your phone. If you have a website, we make sure it says clearly what you do and where you work.

Can you promise I’ll be at the top of Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google decides rankings. We focus on what you can control: a complete, accurate, active profile with real reviews and fast replies.

Are the reviews real?

Always. We only ask your actual customers, we never offer anything in exchange, and we never write, buy or fake reviews.

What is “AI search”?

It’s when people ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok or Google’s AI Overviews for a recommendation instead of scrolling a list of links. Those tools pull from what’s published about you, so clear, consistent information matters.

What area do you serve?

Palatka and all of Putnam County, Florida, including East Palatka, Crescent City, Interlachen, Welaka, Pomona Park, San Mateo, Satsuma and nearby communities.

Who will I work with?

Kyle Simmons, the founder, a Marine Corps veteran who lives in the Palatka area. No call centers and no hand-offs.
