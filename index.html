FAQ

Good questions.

How much does it cost?

The Google check is free. A one-time Google Business Profile cleanup is $299. The monthly plan is $175 a month, month to month. To stop, give 30 days’ notice. My first 6 monthly clients keep $175 for as long as they stay.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No long contract. The monthly plan is month to month. If it isn’t worth it to you, give 30 days’ notice and you’re done.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. You add Putnam Lead Group as a manager on your Google profile and social pages. You stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. You keep using your phone the way you do now. We handle the setup and the upkeep.

Do I need a website?

Not to start. Most of what we do runs off your Google Business Profile and your phone. If you have a website, we make sure it matches your Google profile.

Can you promise I’ll be at the top of Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google decides rankings. We focus on what you can control: a complete, accurate, active profile with real reviews and fast replies.

Are the reviews real?

Always. We only ask your actual customers, and we never write, buy or fake reviews.

What is “AI search”?

It’s when people ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google’s AI answers for a recommendation instead of scrolling. Those tools pull from what’s published about you, so clear, consistent info on your Google profile, website and listings matters. Try the 2-minute test.

What area do you serve?

Palatka and all of Putnam County, FL, including East Palatka, Interlachen, Crescent City, Welaka, San Mateo, Pomona Park, Satsuma and nearby towns.
