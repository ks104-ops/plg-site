FAQ

Good questions.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing for 90 days. At the end, you decide whether to keep going. No pressure either way.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. You keep using your phone the way you do now. We handle the setup and the upkeep.

Do I need a website?

Not to start. Most of what we do runs off your Google Business Profile and your phone. If you have a website, we can add after-hours chat to it.

Can you promise I’ll be at the top of Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google decides rankings. We focus on what you can control: a complete, accurate, active profile with real reviews and fast replies.

Are the reviews real?

Always. We only ask your actual customers, and we never write, buy or fake reviews.

What is “AI search”?

It’s when people ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Grok for a recommendation instead of scrolling Google. Those tools pull from what’s published about you, so clear, consistent info matters.

What area do you serve?

Palatka and the rest of Putnam County, FL, plus nearby communities.
