Review questions

Good questions.

Do you write or buy reviews?

Never. Every review comes from a real customer. Fake reviews break Google’s rules and the law, and they can get a profile penalized.

Can I give a discount for a review?

No. Offering anything in exchange for a review breaks Google’s rules. We make asking easy and polite instead.

Who sends the review request?

You do, from your own phone or email, to customers you’ve finished a job for. We write the short ask and set up the one-tap link. We never text your customers for you.

How do you handle a bad review?

With a calm, professional reply that thanks them, never argues, and offers to make it right offline. Future customers read how you respond as much as the review itself.

Is this included in the price?

The review link and the ask come with the $299 cleanup. Replies to every review are part of the $175 a month plan.
