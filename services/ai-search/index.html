AI search questions

Plain answers.

What is AI search?

It’s when people ask tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, Grok or Google’s AI Overviews a question, like “who’s a good plumber in Palatka?”, and get one written answer instead of a list of links.

Where do AI tools get information about my business?

From what’s published about you: your Google Business Profile, your website, directories like Apple Maps, Bing and Yelp, your reviews, and local news or social posts. When that information is missing or doesn’t match, they may skip you or get you wrong.

What do AEO and GEO mean?

AEO is answer engine optimization and GEO is generative engine optimization. Both mean making your business easy for AI tools to understand, trust and mention. For a local business, it starts with the same basics as Google: accurate, consistent, complete information.

Is this different from regular SEO?

It builds on it. SEO (search engine optimization) helps you show up in Google’s list of results. AI search adds a new place customers ask, and those tools reward clear facts, consistency and real reviews.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No. No one controls what an AI tool says. We make sure the information those tools rely on is clear, correct and consistent, so you’re in the running.
